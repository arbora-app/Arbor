How Arbor Works

Arbor turns raw information into organized knowledge.
Five simple places. One natural flow.

1
Text
Raw information from anywhere.
Articles, notes, books, transcripts, ideas—bring it all in.
2
Soil
Extract meaning from text.
Import or write, then turn it into Leaves using Harvest or Dissect.
3
Leaves
Atomic knowledge you can build with.
Small, focused ideas that are reusable, composable, and easy to connect.
4
Trees
Structure your understanding.
Arrange Leaves into coherent structures—explanations, articles, arguments, plans.
5
Forests
Keep projects and topics together.
All your Trees and Leaves live in one place. Everything stays connected and portable.
